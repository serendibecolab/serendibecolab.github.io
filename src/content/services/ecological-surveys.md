# Ecological Field Surveys

Our field survey teams operate across all of Sri Lanka's major ecosystem types — tropical rainforest, dry-zone scrubland, montane cloud forest, freshwater wetlands, and coastal habitats. We design and execute biodiversity surveys that provide rigorous, quantitative data on species composition, abundance, and habitat condition.

We invite nature enthusiasts, photographers, and aspiring researchers to join our ecological field surveys through guided tours and research trips. Experience the thrill of wildlife photography in the field, learn scientific survey techniques, and contribute to real conservation work.

**Booking is available** for our field survey tours and expeditions. Contact us or visit our booking page to secure your place.

## Survey Types

- **Vegetation surveys**: Quadrat and transect-based assessments of plant community structure and composition
- **Bird surveys**: Point-count and mist-netting methodologies following international standards
- **Herpetological surveys**: Visual encounter surveys, pitfall arrays, and acoustic monitoring for frogs
- **Mammal surveys**: Camera-trap grids and track-station arrays
- **Invertebrate surveys**: Malaise trapping, light trapping, and aquatic kick-sampling

## Who We Work With

We partner with government agencies, conservation NGOs, universities, private landowners, and members of the public who need high-quality biodiversity baseline data for conservation planning, environmental permitting, or research purposes.
