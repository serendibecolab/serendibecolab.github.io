# Conservation Planning

Effective conservation requires more than field data — it requires strategic thinking about where to act, what to protect, and how to allocate limited resources. Serendib Eco Lab brings together biodiversity data, spatial analysis, and stakeholder knowledge to develop conservation plans that are both scientifically robust and practically achievable.

## Our Approach

We use systematic conservation planning tools, including Marxan and Zonation, to identify priority areas for protection and management. Plans are co-developed with communities, government agencies, and other stakeholders to ensure local ownership and long-term implementation.

## Planning Products

- **Species recovery plans** for threatened endemic taxa
- **Protected area management plans** aligned with IUCN green list standards
- **Wildlife corridor strategies** linking fragmented habitat patches
- **Land-use zoning recommendations** for buffer zones and multiple-use landscapes
- **Restoration priority maps** for degraded ecosystems
