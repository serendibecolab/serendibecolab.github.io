# Biodiversity Assessment

Beyond simply listing species, a full biodiversity assessment evaluates the functional health of ecosystems — the interactions between species, the integrity of food webs, and the long-term viability of populations. Serendib Eco Lab offers comprehensive biodiversity assessments that go beyond checklists to deliver ecologically meaningful results.

## What We Assess

- **Species richness and endemism**: Identifying rare, endemic, and threatened species using IUCN criteria
- **Population viability**: Estimating population sizes and modelling long-term persistence
- **Habitat quality**: Assessing vegetation structure, connectivity, and disturbance history
- **Ecosystem services**: Quantifying pollination, water regulation, and carbon storage values

## Deliverables

Clients receive a detailed report including species lists with conservation status, habitat maps, population estimates, threat assessments, and management recommendations aligned with national and international biodiversity frameworks.
