# Wetland Birds — Northern Lagoons and Tanks

The shallow lagoons, estuaries, and ancient irrigation reservoirs (tanks) of northern Sri Lanka provide critical staging and wintering habitat for thousands of migratory waterbirds on the Central Asian Flyway. Our wetland bird surveys document species diversity, population counts, and habitat quality across key sites.

## Methodology

Systematic counts are conducted at fixed watch points during peak migration (October–December) and again during the breeding season (March–May). Breeding colonies are mapped using drone photography, and ring-resighting data contribute to international wader monitoring networks.

## Highlights

Our counts have confirmed internationally significant concentrations of Lesser Sand Plover, Black-tailed Godwit, and Painted Stork at several tank systems. We have also documented breeding records for three globally threatened species at sites not previously known to hold nesting populations.
