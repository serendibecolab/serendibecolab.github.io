# Rainforest Survey — Sinharaja Forest Reserve

Sinharaja is Sri Lanka's last viable area of primary tropical rainforest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Our team conducts regular canopy and understorey surveys to monitor species composition, abundance, and seasonal variation across different forest strata.

## Methodology

We use a combination of point-count transects for birds, pitfall traps for herpetofauna, and botanical quadrats for vegetation assessment. Camera traps placed along wildlife trails provide continuous data on mammal activity.

## Key Findings

Our surveys have recorded over 60 endemic bird species within the reserve, as well as numerous amphibians and reptiles unique to the Sri Lankan wet zone. Continued monitoring reveals long-term trends in population dynamics linked to rainfall patterns and forest fragmentation at the reserve margins.
