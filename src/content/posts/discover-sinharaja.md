# Discover Sinharaja Rainforest

Sinharaja Rainforest Reserve is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the last remaining primary rainforest in Sri Lanka. This ancient forest, dating back over 60 million years, harbors an incredible diversity of flora and fauna.

## Why Sinharaja Matters

- **Endemic Species**: Over 60% of Sri Lanka's endemic species are found here
- **Biodiversity Hotspot**: Home to more than 300 species of trees, 200 bird species
- **Watershed Protection**: Critical water source for surrounding communities
