# About Serendib Eco Lab

Serendib Eco Lab is an ecological research organisation based in Sri Lanka, dedicated to the scientific study and conservation of the island's remarkable natural heritage. "Serendib" — the ancient Arabic name for Sri Lanka — reflects our deep connection to this extraordinary land and its biodiversity.

## Our Mission

We exist to advance the scientific understanding of Sri Lanka's ecosystems and to translate that knowledge into meaningful conservation action. Through rigorous field research, community engagement, and evidence-based advocacy, we work to protect the species and habitats that make Sri Lanka one of the world's most important biodiversity hotspots.

## What We Do

Sri Lanka is home to an exceptional diversity of life. As an island that has been geographically isolated for millions of years, it has evolved a remarkable array of endemic species — plants and animals found nowhere else on Earth. Our work spans the full breadth of the island's ecosystems:

- **Tropical rainforests** — surveying the rich canopy life of Sinharaja and the Knuckles Range
- **Wetlands and waterways** — monitoring aquatic biodiversity and migratory waterbirds
- **Coastal and marine habitats** — assessing coral reef health and beach-nesting turtle populations
- **Dry zone scrublands** — documenting reptiles, invertebrates, and endemic dry-zone flora

## Our Research Approach

Every project we undertake is grounded in sound scientific methodology. We conduct systematic species surveys, long-term population monitoring, habitat mapping using GIS technology, and environmental DNA sampling. Our data is published in peer-reviewed journals and made available to conservation managers, policymakers, and the public.

## Community & Education

We believe that lasting conservation begins with local communities. We run environmental education programmes in schools, train community wildlife monitors, and partner with village communities to develop sustainable land-use practices that benefit both people and nature.
